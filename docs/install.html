Install Da’imun

Free, with no account and no ads. It calls you to every solah with the adhan and keeps your daily chart private on your own device. Choose your device:

MacWindowsPhone

🍎 Mac

macOS 13 (Ventura) or newer · Apple chip or Intel · about 11 MB

Download for Mac · .zip
  1. Open the download.

    Double-click Daimun-mac.zip in your Downloads folder. It turns into the Da’imun app.

  2. Drag Da’imun into your Applications folder.

    Then double-click it there to open it.

  3. You'll see a warning. That's expected. "Da’imun" Not Opened. Apple could not verify "Da’imun" is free of malware…

    Click Done, not Move to Bin. Da’imun is safe; it just isn't registered with Apple yet.

  4. Allow it once.

    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down to the message about "Da’imun" and click Open Anyway. Click Open Anyway again and enter your Mac password if asked.

  5. Choose your town.

    Pick your country, state and city so prayer times are exact. Look for the 🌙 moon in the menu bar at the top of your screen. Click it and turn on Open at login.

✓ That's it. You only allow it once. After that it opens normally and updates itself.

🪟 Windows

Windows 10 or 11 · about 11 MB

Download for Windows · .zip
  1. Extract the download.

    In your Downloads folder, right-click Daimun-windows.zip and choose Extract All… then Extract.

  2. Open the Daimun folder and double-click Istiqamah.exe (the app’s program file).
  3. You may see a blue warning. That's expected. Windows protected your PC

    Click More info, then Run anyway. Da’imun is safe; it just isn't signed with a paid certificate.

  4. It installs itself.

    No admin password needed. Da’imun opens, adds itself to the Start menu and starts with Windows. You can delete the downloaded folder afterwards.

  5. Choose your town.

    Pick your country, state and city. Da’imun lives by the clock: click the ^ arrow in the taskbar if you can't see the 🌙 icon, and drag it next to the clock to keep it in view.

✓ That's it. Close the window and it keeps running by the clock, calling you at each solah. It updates itself.

📱 Phone (or any other computer)

Any phone or browser · nothing to download

Open Da’imun
  1. Open the link above and choose your town.
  2. Add it to your home screen.

    iPhone (Safari): tap Share → Add to Home Screen. Android (Chrome): tap ⋮ → Add to Home screen or Install app.

On phones the tracker and Progress work fully. The adhan plays only while Da’imun is open on screen, so keep your usual phone adhan app for alarms.
